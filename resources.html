When Art Fights Superbugs - Informational (set of 4)

Results of an interdisciplinary art contest: 26 Columbia College Chicago student artists learned about the growing challenge of antimicrobial resistance (AMR). Then each brought their own perspective to creating original public art inspired by what they discovered, offering a glimpse of how a new generation sees one of today's most complex global health threats.

Chicagoland Plant Identification - Coloring Pages (set of 3)

Invasive and native Chicagoland plant identification coloring pages made for the Jefferson Park Pollinator gardens educational access initiative

How it was used: Search and find in gardens, color match the plants found in garden to create your own identification sheet

Age range: Recommended for 4-12th grade.

Food Safety - Mini Workbook (8 Pages)

Schoolhouse mobile kitchen student workbook made for Schoolhouse Kitchen and Studio.

How it was used: Print and staple into personal workbook for students to record and reflect on class activities. Individual pages can also be used as supportive lesson assets.

Age range: Recommended for pre-K through 5th grade.
